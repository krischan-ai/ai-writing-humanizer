# Business Assignment Profile

## Purpose

Guide business case answers and management assignments.

## Preferred structure

Problem → Analysis → Evidence/example → Implication

## Writing style

Prefer:

- practical examples
- clear business impact
- balanced discussion

Avoid:

- empty statements about importance
- repeated corporate buzzwords
- unsupported claims

## Common improvements

Replace:

"Digital transformation plays a crucial role in improving business competitiveness."

With:

"Digital tools can improve efficiency by reducing manual work and helping companies respond faster to customer needs."

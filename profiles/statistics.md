# Statistics Assignment Profile

## Purpose

Guide answers for statistics homework, tutorials, and reports.

## Preferred structure

1. Identify the statistical concept.
2. Apply the method or formula.
3. Explain the result in words.

## Writing style

Prefer:

- clear interpretation
- direct explanation
- connection between numbers and meaning

Avoid:

- repeating textbook definitions
- unexplained formulas
- excessive mathematical wording

## Common answer pattern

For calculation questions:

Formula → Substitution → Result → Interpretation

For experiment questions:

Factor → Treatment → Response variable → Measurement method

## Example improvement

AI style:

"The result demonstrates a statistically significant relationship between the variables."

Student style:

"The test result suggests that the two variables are related under the given significance level."

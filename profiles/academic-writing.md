# Academic Writing Profile

## Use for

- essays
- literature reviews
- reports
- research summaries

## Style goals

Maintain:

- academic accuracy
- clear argument flow
- appropriate formality

Reduce:

- inflated vocabulary
- empty conclusions
- repeated transition phrases

## Preferred structure

Claim

Evidence

Explanation

Connection to research question

## Common AI patterns to remove

Avoid:

"This paper aims to provide a comprehensive analysis..."

Prefer:

"This report examines how X affects Y."

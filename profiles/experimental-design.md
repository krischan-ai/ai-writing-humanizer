# Experimental Design Assignment Profile

## Purpose

Support lab reports and design questions involving factors, treatments, and measurements.

## Preferred structure

Research question → Factor → Treatment → Experimental unit → Response variable → Measurement

## Writing style

Prefer:

- specific variables
- practical measurement descriptions
- simple explanations

Avoid:

- overly theoretical definitions
- copied textbook wording
- unnecessary technical terms

## Common cases

For sensory experiments:

Explain how participants, ratings, or observations are used as measurements.

For controlled experiments:

State what is changed, what is kept constant, and what outcome is measured.

## Example

AI style:

"The treatment represents different experimental conditions applied to evaluate their impact on the response variable."

Student style:

"Each treatment is a different combination of conditions used to compare how the response changes."

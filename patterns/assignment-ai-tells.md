# Assignment AI Writing Tells

Patterns commonly found in AI-generated university answers.

## 1. Generic opening

Problem:

- Starting every answer with a broad definition.
- Example: "In today's society..."

Fix:

Start from the question context or the specific concept being tested.

## 2. Textbook imitation

Problem:

The answer sounds like a copied lecture note instead of a student's explanation.

Fix:

Explain the concept first, then connect it to the given example.

## 3. Excessive completeness

Problem:

AI often lists every possible point even when the question only asks for two or three.

Fix:

Answer the required points directly.

## 4. Artificial academic vocabulary

Avoid unnecessary words:

- utilize
- facilitate
- comprehensive
- significant role
- crucial factor

Prefer normal academic words.

## 5. Perfect symmetry

Problem:

Every paragraph has the same structure.

Fix:

Allow natural variation in sentence length and explanation depth.

## 6. Assignment answer duplication risk

When many students answer the same question:

- avoid identical introduction sentences
- avoid standard three-paragraph structure
- use question-specific examples
- change explanation order when appropriate

## 7. Lab report patterns

Avoid:

"The experiment aims to investigate the important relationship between..."

Prefer:

"This experiment tests whether changing X affects Y."
